## Flagship Project — Systematic Quant Research Pipeline

A personal end-to-end research repo built to learn quantitative research rigorously:

- **Data layer:** source-agnostic (Binance) market data normalized to Parquet.
- **Feature engineering:** log returns, momentum, volatility, z-scores, and calendar features.
- **Models:** gradient-boosted machine-learning classifiers predicting return *direction*.
- **Evaluation:** honest walk-forward (in-sample / out-of-sample) evaluation rather than random splits.
- **Backtesting engine:** full simulation with transaction costs, Sharpe ratio, and max-drawdown.
- **Production learning:** live WebSocket forecasting loop, ML model registry, and statistical drift monitoring.
- **Foundational research:** a from-scratch MiniGPT transformer to understand LLM architecture.

**Researcher's note (intellectual honesty):** Raw momentum features produce ~52% directional accuracy that does not cleanly beat buy-and-hold after transaction costs. The project's value is the *pipeline itself* — a principled method for evaluating whether a signal earns its costs. Building and measuring it is the research mindset; neutralization and correlation analysis are the next steps.
